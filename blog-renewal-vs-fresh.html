UP Scholarship Renewal vs Fresh: Which Form Should You Fill?

Every session, many students face one confusing choice at the portal: renewal or fresh? Choose the wrong one, and the form can get stuck or even rejected. This guide explains up scholarship renewal vs fresh in plain, easy words — who should renew, who must apply fresh, what details carry over, and which mistakes to avoid. Please note: this is an independent student guide, not the official portal. All forms are filled only on the official website, scholarship.up.gov.in.

Who Counts as a Renewal Student?

Renewal is for students who took the UP Scholarship last year and have now moved to the next year of the same course. If you received the scholarship in 2025-26 and are now studying in the next class or year of that same course, you are a renewal student. The renewal form is shorter than the fresh form, because your old details are already with the portal, and you open it with your previous year's registration number. One condition applies to almost everyone: usually about 75% attendance and passing (promotion) to the next year are required. For 2026-27, the renewal dates are given in our Last Date guide — still, check the official portal for the latest update, as dates are final only as shown after login.

Who Must Fill a Fresh Application?

Fill a fresh application in these cases:

  • First-time students: you are taking admission in a new course and have never applied before — you start with a new registration.
  • You changed your college: generally, with a new institute the fresh application rule applies. Confirm this from your institute once.
  • You changed your course: a renewal is meant for the next year of the same course. A changed course normally means a fresh form.
  • You did not apply last year: renewal login works with the previous year's registration number. If you have no such number, you simply cannot renew — apply fresh.

Every fresh application needs an OTR number first. If you do not know what that is, read our article on what is OTR in UP Scholarship, and then follow the Online Form guide for the full fresh process.

What Carries Over in a Renewal — and What You Update

In a renewal, your old information comes already filled. You do not type everything again. The steps are few:

  1. Choose Renewal Login of your category. Remember: the renewal form does not open from Fresh Login.
  2. Log in with your previous year's registration number and your date of birth. Your old details will appear already filled.
  3. Update only the new-year details: your class or year, your previous exam result, and the new fee receipt.
  4. Take the final print and submit the hard copy at your institute.

You can see the complete process in our UP Scholarship Renewal guide.

What Cannot Be Changed in a Renewal Form

Your basic personal details come pre-filled from last year's form, and they stay as they are. The bigger rule is the same for every form: after Final Submit, the form cannot be edited. Changes are possible only when the department opens its correction window. This is why you should read the print preview carefully — twice — before pressing Final. Take special care of the bank account number: if your bank account has changed since last year, do not leave the old number unchecked — the account number is one of the details students most often get wrong. And if last year's payment was stopped because of an NPCI or suspect data problem, fix that cause first — the same problem will stop you this year too.

Good to Know: Common Renewal Mistakes

  • Wrong previous-year registration number: without the correct number, renewal login will not work — so keep last year's final print safe.
  • Logging in from Fresh Login: the renewal form opens only from Renewal Login of your own category.
  • Renewing after changing college or course: a common reason a renewal is rejected — a fresh form is generally the right route.
  • Attendance or result problems: low attendance, failing the previous year, or last year's form not being verified — the most common reasons a renewal gets stuck.
  • Forgetting the hard copy: after the final print, the hard copy must reach your institute. Without it, the form does not move forward.

Frequently Asked Questions

I changed my college. Can I renew?

Generally, no — with a new institute, the fresh application rule applies. Still, confirm your case from your institute before filling the wrong form.

Last year's scholarship money did not come. Can I still fill a renewal?

Yes, you can fill the form. But first find out why last year's status was stopped. If the reason was an NPCI or suspect data problem, it can block this year's form too, so settle that cause first.

I failed in the previous year. Will my renewal work?

Usually renewal needs you to be passed and promoted to the next year. Failing is one of the common reasons a renewal gets stuck. Do not guess — explain your case to your institute's Nodal Officer and fill the form only as advised.

The short rule to remember: same course, same institute, scholarship taken last year — renewal. Almost everything else — fresh. When you are still in doubt, ask the Nodal Officer of your institute before filling, because a wrongly filled renewal is rejected. For final dates and rules, always check the official portal scholarship.up.gov.in for the latest update.

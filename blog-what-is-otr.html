What Is OTR in UP Scholarship? One Time Registration in Simple Words

If you are filling the UP Scholarship form, one word will stop you again and again: OTR. Many students ask what is OTR in UP Scholarship and why the portal does not let them move ahead without it. Here we explain OTR in easy words — what it is, why it became compulsory, and how it saves your time every year. One thing first: this website is an independent student guide, not the official portal. OTR, forms and login work only on scholarship.up.gov.in.

OTR in Simple Words

OTR means One Time Registration. Think of it as your permanent student profile on the portal: you create it only once, and your basic details are saved in it after verification with your Aadhaar card. From the session 2025-26, OTR is compulsory for every UP Scholarship student — without an OTR number, scholarship registration and login do not open. One student gets only one OTR in a lifetime.

OTR is created in three stages, which you can see as tabs on the screen:

  1. Mobile Verification: choose your category, enter the mobile number linked with your Aadhaar, and verify the OTP.
  2. e-KYC: enter your Aadhaar number and complete the verification, with DigiLocker where it is asked.
  3. Final OTR: fill your father's name, mother's name and email, exactly as they are in your certificates, and submit.

After the final submit, your OTR number appears on the screen. Take a screenshot and also write it in a notebook. You can read the full process with pictures in our OTR Registration guide.

Why Has OTR Become Compulsory?

Earlier, students typed the same basic details every year, and a small spelling difference between the form and the marksheet was enough to create trouble. OTR solves this in three simple ways:

  • Your identity is verified once, through Aadhaar e-KYC.
  • The same saved details go into every form, so your name and date of birth stay the same everywhere.
  • Mistakes become less, because the information is stored only after it is verified one time.

That is why OTR was made compulsory — one small effort that protects every form you fill later.

What Details Does Your OTR Store?

Your OTR keeps the basic details that never change from year to year:

  • Your name and date of birth, exactly as in your certificates
  • Your father's name and mother's name
  • Your category
  • The mobile number linked with your Aadhaar
  • Your email address
  • Your Aadhaar-verified identity

Remember one important thing: OTR is made from your Aadhaar. So if your name is wrong in the OTR, the correction first happens in Aadhaar. After that, the OTR is updated through the department's process.

How OTR Makes Your Yearly Form and Renewal Faster

Once your OTR is ready, every year's work becomes smaller. The same OTR number works for a fresh application and for a renewal — you never make a new one. At registration, you simply enter your OTR number and move ahead; without it, the registration does not go forward. Less typing also means fewer mistakes. If you are confused between the two yearly forms, read our guide on UP Scholarship renewal vs fresh application.

OTR Is Not the Same as the Yearly Application Form

Many students think that making the OTR means the scholarship work is done. It is not. OTR is only your identity profile. The real scholarship form is filled separately every year. Here is the simple difference:

PointOTRYearly Application Form
How oftenOnce in a lifetimeEvery year
What it holdsYour verified basic detailsThis year's class, course, fees and documents
Why it is neededFor registration and loginTo actually claim the scholarship

So the order is always the same: first OTR, then registration, then the yearly form. Our Online Form guide explains the full form process step by step.

Good to Know: Common OTR Mistakes

  • Leaving it for the last day: without an OTR number, your registration will not move ahead, so make it early.
  • Using a mobile number not linked with Aadhaar: the OTP and e-KYC will fail. An old or closed number in Aadhaar is a common reason for e-KYC errors — update your mobile number in Aadhaar first, then try again.
  • Making a second OTR: if you forget your number, do not create a new one. Two OTRs can get your form stopped. Use the "Forgot OTR?" option on the OTR login page — with your Aadhaar number and OTP, your number comes back on the screen.
  • Spelling that does not match your marksheet: fill the final OTR details exactly as they are in your school or college certificates.
  • Sharing your OTP: never share the OTP with anyone. It is only for your own verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to make OTR again every year?

No. OTR is made only once in a lifetime. Every year, you log in with the same OTR number, and both fresh and renewal applications work with it.

I forgot my OTR number. What should I do?

Open the OTR login page and click "Forgot OTR?". Enter your Aadhaar number, verify the OTP and submit with the captcha. Your OTR number will appear on the screen. Note it down safely this time.

My name is wrong in the OTR. Can it be corrected?

Yes, but in the right order. Because the OTR is made from Aadhaar, first get the correction done in your Aadhaar. After that, the OTR is updated through the department's process.

OTR is a small one-time job, but it stands behind every scholarship form you will ever fill. Make it carefully, with your own Aadhaar and your own mobile number, and keep the number safe. After that, your yearly form and renewal both become much easier. For dates and rules, always check the official portal scholarship.up.gov.in for the latest update.

UP Scholarship Documents Required: Prepare and Check Everything Before You Apply

Every year, many students start the form and then run around searching for papers. Do not do that. The up scholarship documents required for 2026-27 are simple to collect, but only if you collect them before you sit down to apply. One missing paper, or one wrong spelling, can stop your whole form. This guide gives you the full list, tells you what to check on each paper, and ends with a self-check you can finish in ten minutes. Please note: this is an independent guide site, not the official portal. Forms, login and status work only on scholarship.up.gov.in.

The Full List of Documents You Need

Keep these papers ready in one folder. You can also tick them off, one by one, on our UP Scholarship documents checklist page. Some papers are for every student, and some are only for some students — we have marked those clearly.

  • Aadhaar card — your mobile number should be linked to it.
  • Birth certificate of the student.
  • UP domicile certificate — it proves that you belong to Uttar Pradesh.
  • Income certificate — a fresh one; below we explain why "fresh" matters.
  • Caste certificate — for SC, ST and OBC students.
  • Marksheet of your previous class.
  • Fee receipt of this year, from your school or college.
  • Bank passbook copy — the account should be Aadhaar-seeded and NPCI-mapped.
  • Passport-size photo — recent and clear.
  • Previous year's registration number — only for renewal students.
  • College enrolment number — only for Dashmottar (post-matric) students.
  • OTR number — needed for registration and login on the portal.

Fresh and Renewal: a Small Difference

If you are applying for the first time, you need the full list above. If you are renewing, three things matter most: the marksheet of your new result, the new fee receipt, and correct bank details. And if the validity of your income certificate has ended, get a new one made before you apply.

The Golden Rule: Your Name Must Match Everywhere

Now the most important check of all. Your name must be spelled in exactly the same way on four papers: your Aadhaar card, your marksheet, your bank account and your income certificate. Even a small difference — like "Kumar" written in one paper and missing in another — can turn your data into "Suspect Data" on the portal. Suspect data means delay, extra checking, and sometimes rejection. So compare the spellings letter by letter, today itself. Check your date of birth and your father's name in the same careful way. If anything does not match, get it corrected first, and only then fill the form.

Income Certificate and Caste Certificate: How They Are Made

These two certificates cause the most delays, so start with them. The income certificate can be applied for online through UP's e-District or Jansunwai services, and it is also made at the tehsil. It takes time, so apply well before the form's last date — not in the last week. Remember, a fresh income certificate is usually asked for, and every certificate has a validity limit. An old, expired one is a common reason for trouble. The caste certificate is compulsory if you belong to SC or ST, and OBC students need it too. If you do not have yours yet, start the process early, for the same reason: it is not made in one day. Which caste and income papers you need also depends on your category, so read our UP Scholarship eligibility guide once.

Good to Know: Common Mistakes That Delay Forms

  • Filling the form first, searching for papers later. Keep the full folder ready before you start.
  • Using a parent's bank account. The student's own account is usually needed, because the money comes through DBT, linked to the student's Aadhaar.
  • Using an old income certificate whose validity has ended. Get a fresh one made.
  • Different name spellings on different papers. A small difference can create a big delay.
  • Renewal students forgetting the new marksheet and the new fee receipt.

Final Self-Check Before You Submit

One evening before you apply, sit with your folder and go through these steps:

  1. All the papers that apply to you are in one folder, originals and copies together.
  2. Your name's spelling is the same on Aadhaar, marksheet, bank account and income certificate.
  3. Your income certificate is fresh and within its validity.
  4. Your bank account is your own, Aadhaar-seeded and NPCI-mapped.
  5. Your OTR number — and registration or enrolment number, if they apply — are written down and correct.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the income certificate made online?

Yes. You can apply through UP's e-District or Jansunwai services, and it is also made at the tehsil. It takes some time, so get it made before the form dates come close.

Can I use my mother's or father's bank account?

Usually, no. The student's own bank account is needed, because the scholarship money comes through DBT, which is linked to the student's Aadhaar.

One paper has my name spelled differently. What should I do?

Get it corrected before you fill the form. A spelling difference between your Aadhaar, marksheet, bank account and income certificate can create Suspect Data and delay your scholarship.

Documents look boring, but they decide everything. Spend one hour today collecting and checking your papers, and your form will take minutes, not days. If you want to understand how family income is judged, read our guide on the UP Scholarship income limit. And when you are fully ready, apply only on the official portal, scholarship.up.gov.in — and always confirm the latest update there.
